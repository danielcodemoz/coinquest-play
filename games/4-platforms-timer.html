Level 4 · Moving Platforms & Timer

← → or A D to move · Space, ↑ or W to jump · R to restart · Falling costs 5 seconds

What you are learning

Platforms can move by themselves and carry the player. A timer counts time, and a variable can be shown on screen as a countdown.

Events

  • ConditionAt every frame (while playing)
    ActionTime left = 50 − value of scene timer “level”
  • ConditionPlayer stands on a moving Platform
    ActionThe player is carried along automatically
  • ConditionPlayer Y > bottom of the screen
    ActionMove player to last safe ground · add 5 s to the timer
  • ConditionTime left ≤ 0
    ActionChange scene to Game Over

Variables

TimeLeft is calculated from a scene timer. platformX shows where the first platform is: it goes back and forth between two points.

Your turn in GDevelop

  • Action “Start (or reset) a scene timer”; read it with TimerElapsedTime("level").
  • Show the countdown with a Text object.
