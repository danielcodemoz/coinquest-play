Level 3 Enemies & Lives

What you are learning

You have 3 lives (a variable). Conditions can be combined: colliding AND falling means a stomp; colliding alone means ouch.

Events

  • ConditionPlayer collides with Slime AND Player is falling
    ActionDelete Slime · add 50 to Score · bounce
  • ConditionPlayer collides with Slime AND timer “hit” ≥ 1.5 s
    ActionSubtract 1 from Lives · reset timer “hit”
  • ConditionTimer “hit” < 1.5 s
    ActionMake the player blink (invincible)
  • ConditionVariable Lives ≤ 0
    ActionChange scene to Game Over

Why the invincible timer?

A collision event runs every frame (60 times a second). Without a short invincibility, one touch would remove all your lives in a blink!

Your turn in GDevelop

  • Create Lives = 3. Add a Slime with an object variable direction.
  • Patrol: move the slime, flip direction when it hits a wall.
  • Use sub-events: if falling → stomp, else → lose a life.
  • Draw hearts with a Tiled Sprite or one Text object.
