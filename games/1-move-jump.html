Level 1 Move & Jump

What you are learning

Every frame, GDevelop checks your events. When a condition is true, its actions run. Watch the events light up while you play.

Events

  • ConditionLeft key is pressed
    ActionPlatformer character: simulate left key press
  • ConditionRight key is pressed
    ActionPlatformer character: simulate right key press
  • ConditionSpace key is pressed
    ActionPlatformer character: simulate jump key press
  • ConditionPlayer is not on the floor (jumping or falling)
    ActionGravity keeps pulling the player down
  • ConditionPlayer collides with Flag
    ActionShow “Level complete”

Variables

The live values under the game (speedX, speedY, onFloor) are numbers the Platformer character behavior keeps for you.

Try it: behavior properties

These match the properties of the Platformer character behavior.

Your turn in GDevelop

  • Give your Player the Platformer character behavior and your ground the Platform behavior.
  • Tune Gravity, Jump speed and Max speed until it feels good.
  • Add a Flag and an event: Player collides with Flag → change scene.
